# Reel variations of `promo-elementos`, 10 Nov 2026

Sixteen 9:16 variations (`b` to `q`) of the reel `promo-elementos` (`a`), which is built around the 16 generated cutouts
described in [`generated-cutouts.md`](generated-cutouts.md). All run at 120 BPM, 30 fps, 1080 x 1920, rendered in After
Effects from baked specs and encoded with x264 at CRF 18. Each variation has its own scene order, scene lengths, palette
sequence, hero and side cutouts, wall order and random seed. Copy on screen comes only from the asset sheet, plus the
address and the venue handle. The masters, check sheets and upload copies are not in this repository.

## Checks

| Check | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | Four frames per scene from a quick compositor, before rendering | No hidden or clipped element. The title hero covers "Fiesta" for about four frames while it lands |
| Master | 1 fps contact sheet per clip | Matches the layout sheets |
| Colour | Four consecutive frames of the upload copy on a held stretch | Largest frame-to-frame change 0.20 to 0.57 |
| Cover | Frame from the master with the title or lockup visible and all stickers landed | `g`, `h`: frame 442 used, because 436 lacked the side stickers and 446 had the transition tape |
