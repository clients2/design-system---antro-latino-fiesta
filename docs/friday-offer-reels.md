# Antro Brixton Friday offer reels, 6 Nov 2026

Five vertical promo reels (1080 × 1920) for the Friday bottomless offer at Antro Brixton, made with Claude Opus 5 on
Friday 6 Nov 2026 from three photos of the bar. The offer text came from antrobars.com/bottomless-nights. The videos are
not in this repository.

These reels came before the `latino-fiesta` design system and do not use it.
